Put the Boromir "It's a gift" photo in this folder.

Any filename works, as long as it is a .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp, .gif or .avif.
If there are several images, one with "boromir" in its name is used.
No restart is needed. `npm start` prints whether Boromir was found.

Once it's here, Boromir shows up now and then:
- sometimes he peeks in from the corner of any page,
- sometimes he joins the "Reveal my quest" moment,
- he always appears when you click the "It's a gift." quote in the footer,
- add ?boromir to any URL to summon him on purpose.
